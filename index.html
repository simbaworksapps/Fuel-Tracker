Quick Notes

  • Yellow boxes mark the published cells used. Two or four boxes indicate interpolation.
  • TAS uses standard atmosphere; receiver altitude is modeled 1,000 feet below tanker altitude.
  • Turn range and offset use the 25° AOB tables and ARIP-to-ARCP course drift.
  • Chart timing is nil wind. Wind-symbol timing in the calculator is supplemental.
  • A yellow ~ marks limited extrapolation; farther values display OUT OF RANGE.
